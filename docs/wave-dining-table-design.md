# The Wave

## Design intent

The Wave keeps the 75 × 35.5 × 29.5 in Hover-table envelope, profiled oak top, three recessed widthwise C-channels, and two lengthwise upper rails. It replaces each closed transverse end box with an open frame made from one sculpted top rail and two full-height legs.

The top rail owns both the outer and inner cubic Bézier returns. Those returns meet each leg at square tangent seams, creating the recognizable wave-shaped shoulder while keeping the leg blank straight-grained and independently fabricable. The opening continues to the floor; there is no hidden bottom rail.

## Default assembly

- 1 profiled oak tabletop
- 3 flush blackened-steel C-channels
- 2 mirrored wave-curve top rails
- 4 full-height oak legs
- 2 parallel lengthwise upper rails
- no lower lengthwise member
- no default leveling hardware

The default cut list therefore contains 12 finished pieces across five schedule lines. The top and leg routing-template families come from the same B1 and B3 profiles used by the assembled model, exploded view, and cut list.

## Joinery and load path

The lengthwise rails bear directly against the tabletop underside and terminate flush against the inside faces of the end frames. The leg-to-top-rail seams should be treated as structural joinery rather than decorative butt joints; the model preserves the finished profiles but does not prescribe a tested joint. Loose tenons, housed mortise-and-tenon joinery, or another verified equivalent should be evaluated with full-scale mockups before stock is committed.

Removing the bottom rails makes the table visually lighter but reduces transverse frame closure and floor-level torsion. The in-app structure panel applies an explicit open-frame penalty and should be read only as a geometry comparison, not engineering certification. A physical corner-racking mockup and a loaded full-base test remain required before a furniture build.
